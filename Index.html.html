About IOTA Tech

We at IOTA Tech work to create simplified and easily understandable lab videos with calculation and concepts involved in each practical experiment, for students across all streams.

This library started as a small student project — one built out of the same frustration every lab student knows: finding a video for your experiment that actually explains the concept and the calculation, instead of just filming an apparatus and hoping you can figure out the rest.

Every video here has been picked (or filmed by us) with that in mind. No comment sections to get lost in, no autoplay pulling you toward something unrelated — just the experiment, the method, and the numbers behind it.

It's free to use for any student, in any stream, at any level — school, PU, or engineering. If it saves you twenty minutes before a practical exam, it's done its job.

Know a better video for one of these experiments, spotted something missing, or just have feedback on the site itself? Write to us at infoiotatech@gmail.com — video suggestions, reviews, and change requests are all welcome.
